Ritual 16 of 20 · 12 minutes · Focus: Full Face

The longest ritual of the plan at twelve minutes — a full-face calm-down for the evening. Massage, drainage, tongue work and lip care, finished with a slow seal.

The routine

9 exercises, 12 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Facial Massage — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand comfortably with your shoulders down.
  2. Rest your fingertips on your cheeks, close to the jawline.
  3. Press lightly and slide your fingers up and out toward your ears.
  4. Keep the motion smooth and steady.

Lymphatic Nodes Activation (Eyebrows) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture.
  2. Place your index fingertips just above the inner corners of your brows.
  3. Press softly and make small circles.

Deep Fascia Release for Cheek Muscle (Right) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture.
  2. Pinch your right cheek near the nostril gently with your left thumb, index and middle fingers.
  3. Use your right fingers to pinch the cheek next to your left hand.
  4. Move your fingers up and down along the cheekbone in a soft pinching motion.
  5. Keep working across the cheek, area by area.

Deep Fascia Release for Cheek Muscle (Left) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture.
  2. Pinch your left cheek near the nostril gently with your right thumb and index fingers.
  3. Use your left fingers to pinch the cheek next to your right hand.
  4. Move your fingers up and down along the cheekbone in a soft pinching motion.
  5. Keep working across the cheek, area by area.

Pulled Tongue (Alternating) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Tilt your head slowly to one side, bringing your ear toward your shoulder.
  3. Stick your tongue out and point it down toward the same side, holding the stretch briefly.
  4. Return your head to the centre and relax your tongue.
  5. Repeat on the other side, alternating slowly and with control.

Lymphatic Nodes Activation (Armpits) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Put your right hand under your left armpit and pump the area gently.
  3. Switch sides and repeat with your left hand under your right armpit.
  4. Then cross your arms and massage both armpits at the same time.

Lip Plumper — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders and your head straight.
  2. Stick your tongue out just a little.
  3. Hold the position with your facial muscles engaged.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

A wind-down: your whole face should feel soft, warm and sleepy-calm. This is the one to do before bed.
