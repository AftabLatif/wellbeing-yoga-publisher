Ritual 5 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Eyes

Screens, stress and squinting all settle around the eyes and brows. This nine-minute ritual smooths the brow, opens the eyelids and circles the temples to let that tension go.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

V Shape Release for Eyes (Right) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand comfortably.
  2. Make a "V" with the index and middle fingers of both hands.
  3. Use your left hand’s fingers to hold the right side of your face gently.
  4. With your right hand, press softly and glide your fingers upward toward your hairline.
  5. Keep the gliding motion smooth and continuous.

V Shape Release for Eyes (Left) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand comfortably.
  2. Make a "V" with the index and middle fingers of both hands.
  3. Use your right hand’s fingers to hold the left side of your face gently.
  4. With your left hand, press softly and glide your fingers upward toward your hairline.
  5. Keep the gliding motion smooth and continuous.

Eyelid Opener — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Rest your middle and ring fingers on your temples, just outside the outer ends of your brows.
  3. Lift the skin gently upward with your fingers.
  4. Look down and move your eyes slowly from side to side, feeling gentle resistance from your fingers.
  5. Repeat slowly and with control, keeping your face relaxed.

Eye Flex — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your index fingers just below the outer ends of your brows and your middle fingers on your cheekbones.
  3. Pull the skin around your eyes gently outward to create light resistance.
  4. Hold for a moment.
  5. Release slowly and repeat with controlled movements.

Temple Circles — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place the pads of your index and middle fingers on your temples.
  3. Apply soft, light pressure.
  4. Move your fingers in slow, small circles.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your brow and temples should feel lighter, as if a tight band has loosened. Your eyes should feel rested and less heavy.
