Ritual 20 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Neck

The last ritual of the plan — and the gentlest. Nine slow minutes of kissing stretches, neck work and jaw release to close out the 28 days.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Criss Cross Air Kissing — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with your hands on the sides of your shoulders.
  2. Turn your head left, tilt it slightly up and pucker your lips toward the ceiling, feeling the stretch in your neck and jawline.
  3. Return to centre.
  4. Repeat on the other side and keep alternating.

Neck Stretch (Alternating) — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Cross your hands flat over your upper chest, one palm resting directly over the back of the other, to anchor the skin.
  3. Tilt your head gently back to stretch the front of your neck.
  4. Turn your head slowly to one side while shifting your hands slightly toward the opposite collarbone, then repeat on the other side.
  5. Keep alternating sides in a smooth, continuous movement.

Jaw Depuffing — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit comfortably with an upright posture and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Curl your index and middle fingers into bent "hooks" on both hands.
  3. Place your jawbone firmly between the knuckles of your index finger (above) and middle finger (below).
  4. Sweep your knuckles gently outward along your jaw toward your ears, alternating sides.

Tongue Side — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight posture.
  2. Press your tongue firmly against the inside of one cheek.
  3. Slide your tongue slowly across to the other cheek.
  4. Repeat the side-to-side motion smoothly.

Cheeks Relaxer — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Make an "O" shape with your lips, then move your lips toward one side until you feel a gentle stretch in that cheek.
  3. Hold briefly, then move your lips to the other side to stretch the opposite cheek.
  4. Keep alternating sides at a steady, controlled pace.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

The perfect closer: your jaw and neck should feel loose, warm and ready for sleep. End the plan the way you started it — gently.
