Ritual 17 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Smile

Most smiles are slightly uneven, and that is completely normal. This nine-minute ritual works both sides of your smile so it feels balanced and easy.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Smile Lifter — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Lift your chin slowly toward the ceiling until you are looking upward.
  3. Pull the corners of your mouth into a wide smile, keeping your lips covering your teeth.
  4. Hold the smile briefly, feeling the stretch in your neck and along your jawline.

Smile On — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand upright with a relaxed posture.
  2. Place your index fingers softly at the corners of your mouth.
  3. Use your fingers to lift the corners of your lips while you smile as widely as you can, showing your teeth.
  4. Then guide the corners of your lips inward with your fingers as you purse them into a tight pucker, pushing them forward.
  5. Alternate slowly between the smile and the pucker, keeping the motion controlled.

Exaggerated Smile — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall, shoulders relaxed.
  2. Clench your teeth lightly, stretch your lips into a wide smile and nudge your lower jaw slightly forward.
  3. Hold for two seconds and feel the gentle stretch along your jawline and neck.
  4. Slide your jaw slowly back while keeping the smile and the light clench, then return to the start and repeat the forward-and-back movement.

Lymphatic Nodes Activation Bridge — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your index fingertips on either side of the bridge of your nose, near the inner corners of your eyes.
  3. Press gently and steadily, making small circles.
  4. Keep the circular motions going while you breathe deeply and hold light pressure.

Lip Plumper — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders and your head straight.
  2. Stick your tongue out just a little.
  3. Hold the position with your facial muscles engaged.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your smile should feel even and easy on both sides. Don’t chase perfection — a natural, relaxed smile is the goal.
