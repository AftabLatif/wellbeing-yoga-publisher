Ritual 19 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Lips

A soft evening session for cheeks and lips. Circles, sculpting and a slow mouth massage to end the day with a relaxed, calm face.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Nasolabial Circles — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your index fingertips on the sides of your nose.
  3. Press gently and steadily.
  4. Massage in small, outward circles.

Cheek Lifting and Sculpting — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Rest your palms on your cheekbones with your fingers loosely curled.
  3. Drop your jaw into a long "O" shape with your mouth.
  4. Press firmly but gently and lift your cheeks.
  5. Hold for a moment and feel the tension.

The Yummy Face with Hands — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your index fingertips lightly on the corners of your mouth.
  3. Stick your tongue out and slide it slowly along your upper lip from one side to the other.
  4. Keep gentle resistance with your fingers and repeat.

Press Impress — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture, shoulders down.
  2. Hold one corner of your mouth softly between the thumb and index finger of one hand.
  3. With the other hand, gently press your upper and lower lips together, moving from one side of your mouth to the other.
  4. Swap hands and repeat on the other side.
  5. Keep the motion smooth and controlled.

Mouth Area Massage — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place the index and middle fingers of both hands horizontally, above and below your lips.
  3. Press gently and glide your fingers slowly outward toward the corners of your mouth.
  4. Lift your fingers, return to the start, and repeat the motion smoothly.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your cheeks and lips should feel soft and calm, ready for the night. Keep the pressure at its lightest here.
