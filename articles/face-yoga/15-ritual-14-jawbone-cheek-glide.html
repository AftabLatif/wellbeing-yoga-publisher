Ritual 14 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Jawline

Gliding strokes along the jaw and deep, gentle pinching through the cheeks. This nine-minute ritual is especially good if you clench or grind your jaw.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Jawbone to Nose Glides (Right) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall, spine straight, shoulders relaxed.
  2. Place your right index and middle fingers vertically on your jawline below the corner of your mouth.
  3. Put your left index finger horizontally across your cheek, fingertip near the base of your nostril.
  4. Press gently and glide your left index finger upward toward your nose.
  5. Lift, return to the start, and repeat.

Jawbone to Nose Glides (Left) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall, spine straight, shoulders relaxed.
  2. Place your left index and middle fingers vertically on your jawline below the corner of your mouth.
  3. Put your right index finger horizontally across your cheek, fingertip near the base of your nostril.
  4. Press gently and glide your right index finger upward toward your nose.
  5. Lift, return to the start, and repeat.

Deep Fascia Release for Cheek Muscle (Right) — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture.
  2. Pinch your right cheek near the nostril gently with your left thumb, index and middle fingers.
  3. Use your right fingers to pinch the cheek next to your left hand.
  4. Move your fingers up and down along the cheekbone in a soft pinching motion.
  5. Keep working across the cheek, area by area.

Deep Fascia Release for Cheek Muscle (Left) — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture.
  2. Pinch your left cheek near the nostril gently with your right thumb and index fingers.
  3. Use your left fingers to pinch the cheek next to your right hand.
  4. Move your fingers up and down along the cheekbone in a soft pinching motion.
  5. Keep working across the cheek, area by area.

Bumblebee Lips — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Breathe in deeply through your nose.
  3. Breathe out fully through your mouth with a lip trill — keep your lips loose so they vibrate together like a motorboat, a soft "brrr" sound.
  4. Keep breathing in through your nose and out through your mouth with the lip trill going.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your jaw and cheeks should feel glided and smooth, with less tightness along the jawline. If you grind your teeth, this one is your friend.
