Ritual 2 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Eyes

This nine-minute ritual is all about the eyes. Small, careful movements around the brows, crow’s feet and forehead help tired morning eyes look brighter and feel more open.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Lymphatic Nodes Activation (Eyebrows) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a relaxed posture.
  2. Place your index fingertips just above the inner corners of your brows.
  3. Press softly and make small circles.

Crow Feet Press — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with your head level and shoulders relaxed.
  2. Put the index and middle fingers of both hands at the outer corners of your eyes, thumbs resting under your chin.
  3. Press gently and draw the skin slightly outward and upward.
  4. Hold while your face stays soft and relaxed.

Crow's Feet Massage (Right) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Place the fingers of both hands near the outer corner of your right eye, your left hand reaching across your head.
  2. Pull the skin gently outward.
  3. Repeat with slow, controlled strokes.

Crow's Feet Massage (Left) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Place the fingers of both hands near the outer corner of your left eye, your right hand reaching across your head.
  2. Pull the skin gently outward.
  3. Repeat with slow, controlled strokes.

Upward Forehead Sweep — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Lay your palms on your forehead just above your brows, fingers pointing up.
  3. Press lightly and sweep your hands smoothly up toward your hairline.
  4. Lift your hands, return to the start, and repeat.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your eye area should feel fresh and open, like you have just splashed cold water on your face. Keep the pressure feather-light around the eyes — the skin there is thin.
