Ritual 12 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Neck

This ritual is about flow, not force. Gentle pumping around the ears, collarbones and armpits encourages lymphatic drainage, followed by a slow neck stretch.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Lymphatic Nodes Activation (Ears) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with your neck and shoulders relaxed.
  2. Place your index and middle fingers on either side of each ear, index finger behind and middle finger in front.
  3. Press gently and massage around your ears in small circles.
  4. Continue at a slow, steady pace.

Lymphatic Nodes Activation (Collarbone) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your fingers in the hollows just above your collarbones.
  3. Press lightly and rhythmically, downward and inward.
  4. Keep this gentle pumping movement going up and down.

Lymphatic Nodes Activation (Armpits) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Put your right hand under your left armpit and pump the area gently.
  3. Switch sides and repeat with your left hand under your right armpit.
  4. Then cross your arms and massage both armpits at the same time.

Neck Stretch (Alternating) — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Cross your hands flat over your upper chest, one palm resting directly over the back of the other, to anchor the skin.
  3. Tilt your head gently back to stretch the front of your neck.
  4. Turn your head slowly to one side while shifting your hands slightly toward the opposite collarbone, then repeat on the other side.
  5. Keep alternating sides in a smooth, continuous movement.

Tongue to Nose — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Rest one hand gently on your throat.
  3. Open your mouth slightly and reach your tongue up toward your nose.
  4. Hold briefly, release, and repeat.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

You should feel lighter through the neck and collarbone, with a sense of drainage and ease. Move slowly — this one is about gentle flow, not force.
