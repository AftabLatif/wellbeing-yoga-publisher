Ritual 8 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Neck

The chin and neck are often the first places to show tension and slack. This nine-minute ritual lifts the chin, massages the jaw and releases both sides of the neck.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Double Chin Lift — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Lay your hands flat on your shoulders to hold them down.
  3. Tip your chin slowly toward the ceiling and look up.
  4. Stick your tongue out to one side, then sweep it across your upper lip to the other side in a half-circle.
  5. Keep sweeping side to side, feeling the stretch under your chin and through your neck.

Chin Massage — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your thumbs and first two fingers at the centre of your chin.
  3. Pinch gently and sweep your fingers outward along your jawline toward the corners of your lips.
  4. Keep repeating the motion.

Neck Tension Release (Left) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Find the long muscle on the left side of your neck, running from behind your ear to your collarbone.
  3. Pinch it gently between your thumb and index or middle finger.
  4. Work slowly upward along the muscle with soft pressure.
  5. Then work back down the same muscle.

Neck Tension Release (Right) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Find the long muscle on the right side of your neck, running from behind your ear to your collarbone.
  3. Pinch it gently between your thumb and index or middle finger.
  4. Work slowly upward along the muscle with soft pressure.
  5. Then work back down the same muscle.

Jaw Depuffing — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit comfortably with an upright posture and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Curl your index and middle fingers into bent "hooks" on both hands.
  3. Place your jawbone firmly between the knuckles of your index finger (above) and middle finger (below).
  4. Sweep your knuckles gently outward along your jaw toward your ears, alternating sides.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your chin and neck should feel stretched and warm. The muscles under your chin may feel worked — that is the point — but never sore.
