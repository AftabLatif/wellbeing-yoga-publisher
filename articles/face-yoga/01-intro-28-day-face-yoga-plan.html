Face yoga is a set of gentle exercises and massage moves for the muscles of your face, jaw and neck. Just like the rest of your body, your face has dozens of muscles — and they hold tension, get tired, and go slack when they are never used. A few minutes a day of focused movement wakes them up, boosts blood flow, and helps your face look and feel fresher.

This plan gives you 20 short rituals, each one 9 to 12 minutes long. You do one a day, five days a week, with two rest days. No mat, no equipment, no gym — just clean hands, a little face oil and a mirror.

How the plan works

Every ritual follows the same simple shape:

  • Warm up (30 seconds): apply a few drops of face oil so your fingers glide instead of dragging on your skin.
  • The workout (5 to 7 exercises): the main moves for the day, each one 90 seconds to 2 minutes.
  • Cooldown (30 seconds): a slow palm press that seals in the oil and lets your face relax.

Each ritual focuses on a different area — eyes, cheeks, jawline, neck, smile lines — so by the end of the four weeks you have worked your whole face.

The 28-day schedule

Workouts run Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are rest days — your face needs recovery just like any other muscle group.

  • Week 1 — Rituals 1 to 5: Morning Face Activation, Bright-Eyed Morning, Cheek Sculpt & Smile, Jaw & Neck Sculpt, Eye Refresh & Brow Lift
  • Week 2 — Rituals 6 to 10: Midday Lift & Tap, Nasolabial Softening, Double-Chin & Neck Lift, Temple & Brow Softening, Full Cheek Definition
  • Week 3 — Rituals 11 to 15: Full Face Sculpt & Release, Lymphatic Neck Drainage, Quiet Eyes & Forehead, Jawbone & Cheek Glide, Tongue & Jaw Tension Release
  • Week 4 — Rituals 16 to 20: Evening Full-Face Calm, Smile Symmetry, Jawline Contour & Collarbone, Evening Cheek & Lip Calm, Evening Jaw & Neck Wind-Down

Missed a day? Just pick up where you left off — there is no need to double up.

What you need

  • Face oil: any light facial oil you already use. It stops your fingers from pulling on your skin.
  • Clean hands: wash them before every ritual — you will be touching your face for ten minutes.
  • A mirror: so you can see what your hands are doing, especially in the first week.
  • Ten quiet minutes: morning, midday or evening — whatever time you will actually stick to.

A few ground rules

  • Be gentle. The skin on your face is thin, especially around the eyes. Light pressure is enough — if your skin turns red or sore, you are pressing too hard.
  • Stop if it hurts. A mild stretch is fine; sharp pain is not. Skip any move that hurts and move on.
  • Keep it clean. Never do face yoga with dirty hands or over broken skin, spots or rashes.
  • Be patient. Face yoga is a habit, not a quick fix. Most people notice their face feeling more awake and relaxed within the first week, and steadier results over the full 28 days.

Please note

This plan is general wellness information, not medical advice. If you have a skin condition, recent facial surgery, jaw problems, or any medical concern about your face or neck, check with your doctor before starting.
