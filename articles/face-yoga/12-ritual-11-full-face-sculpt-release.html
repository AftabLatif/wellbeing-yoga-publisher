Ritual 11 of 20 · 11 minutes · Focus: Full Face

The most complete session of the plan — eleven minutes that work the whole face, from forehead sweeps to neck massage and eye movements. A full sculpt-and-release day.

The routine

8 exercises, 11 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Effleurage Finish (Alternating) — 2 minutes

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your right palm under your jawline and wrap your left hand over your head to your right cheek.
  3. Glide your lower hand upward toward your ear and your left hand outward toward your hairline.
  4. Swap sides and repeat.

Forehead and Eyebrow Lift Sweep — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Lay the palm of one hand flat against your forehead, covering your brow.
  3. Press gently and sweep your hand upward toward your hairline, lifting the forehead skin and brow.
  4. Release, switch hands, and repeat on the other side of your forehead.
  5. Keep alternating sides with slow, controlled movements.

Cheek Tension — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit up straight with a neutral spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place the sides of your palms softly against your cheeks, fingers pointing upward.
  3. Press your cheeks gently inward with your hands while puckering your lips tightly, as if giving a kiss.
  4. Hold the tension for a few seconds, then release and repeat.

Kiss the Sky — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Cross your arms over your chest and rest your hands on your shoulders.
  3. Tilt your head gently upward and look toward the ceiling.
  4. Pucker your lips upward as if kissing the sky.
  5. Hold for a moment, then relax and return to the start.
  6. Repeat the movement.

Back of the Neck Massage — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your fingertips on the back of your neck.
  3. Press gently and massage in small circles.
  4. Work slowly down toward your shoulders and repeat.

Eyes Up and Down — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders, head facing forward.
  2. Look down toward the floor to begin.
  3. Move your eyes slowly up toward the ceiling as far as comfortable, without moving your head.
  4. Repeat the up-and-down movement smoothly.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Expect an all-over warmth and a feeling of release, especially in your neck and jaw. Finish feeling calm, not tired.
