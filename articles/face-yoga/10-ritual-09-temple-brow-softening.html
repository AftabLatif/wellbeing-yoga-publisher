Ritual 9 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Forehead

If you carry stress in your forehead and temples, this is your ritual. Nine minutes of lifts, smoothers and gentle eye movements to soften the brow and brighten the eyes.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Temple Eyes Lift — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Make a "C" shape with your index fingers and thumbs.
  3. Put your index fingers just above your brows and your thumbs on your cheekbones.
  4. Pull the skin gently outward to create tension.
  5. Look upward while keeping your forehead smooth and relaxed.

Eye Bag Toner — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine.
  2. Open your mouth into an "O" shape, covering your teeth with your lips.
  3. Look up toward the ceiling without moving your head.
  4. Hold the position while your forehead stays relaxed.

Under Eye Smoother — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place the pads of your index and middle fingers softly at the inner corners of your eyes, near the sides of your nose.
  3. With very light pressure, press and glide your fingers outward along your lower eye sockets toward your temples.
  4. Pause briefly at your temples, then glide smoothly back to the start and repeat.

Eyes Up and Down — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders, head facing forward.
  2. Look down toward the floor to begin.
  3. Move your eyes slowly up toward the ceiling as far as comfortable, without moving your head.
  4. Repeat the up-and-down movement smoothly.

Forehead Relief — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place the knuckles of both hands at the centre of your forehead, just above your brows.
  3. Press lightly to moderately and stroke outward toward your temples.
  4. Lift your fingers, return to the centre, and repeat.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your forehead should feel smooth and relaxed, and the tension you carry between your brows should feel eased. Your eyes should feel bright and open.
