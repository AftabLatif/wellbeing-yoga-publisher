Ritual 13 of 20 · 9 minutes · Focus: Eyes

A quiet one. Nine slow minutes for tired eyes and a tight forehead — soft circles, gentle lifts and deep breathing. Do it when your eyes feel heavy.

The routine

7 exercises, 9 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Gentle Eye Zone Awakening — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a neutral posture and relaxed neck and shoulders.
  2. Place the pads of your ring fingers on either side of your nose, just below the inner corners of your eyes.
  3. Press gently along your brow bone, moving outward toward your temples.
  4. Trace lightly under your eyes, sweeping back toward your nose to complete the circle.
  5. Repeat this soothing circular motion in a steady rhythm.

Index Finger Eyeball — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand comfortably.
  2. Lift the inner corners of your brows gently with your index fingers.
  3. Look downward, then slowly bring your gaze straight ahead.
  4. Keep moving your eyes down and back to centre in a smooth, controlled motion.

Lymphatic Nodes Activation Bridge — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Place your index fingertips on either side of the bridge of your nose, near the inner corners of your eyes.
  3. Press gently and steadily, making small circles.
  4. Keep the circular motions going while you breathe deeply and hold light pressure.

Eyebrow Lift — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Tuck your thumbs under the inner ends of your brows and rest your index fingers just above them.
  3. Pinch the brow area softly and slide your fingers outward toward your temples.
  4. As you travel outward, gently raise your brows.
  5. Slide back to the inner corners and repeat.

Eyelid Opener — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Rest your middle and ring fingers on your temples, just outside the outer ends of your brows.
  3. Lift the skin gently upward with your fingers.
  4. Look down and move your eyes slowly from side to side, feeling gentle resistance from your fingers.
  5. Repeat slowly and with control, keeping your face relaxed.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

Your eyes and forehead should feel quiet and rested, like the end of a good nap. Keep everything slow and soft.
