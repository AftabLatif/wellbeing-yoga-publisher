Ritual 6 of 20 · 11 minutes · Focus: Full Face

A midday reset for your face. Two minutes of light tapping wakes up circulation, then eye, cheek and chin work carries you through the afternoon looking fresh.

The routine

8 exercises, 11 minutes in total. Work through them in order, and keep your touch gentle throughout.

Warm Up

Face Oil Application — 30 seconds

  1. Sit up tall with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Put a few drops of facial oil on your fingertips and rub your palms together to warm it.
  3. Press your hands gently onto the middle of your cheeks.
  4. Sweep your fingers upward and outward across your cheeks, jawline and forehead.
  5. Keep going with slow, soft strokes until the oil has absorbed.

The Workout

Full Face Tapping — 2 minutes

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Tap your forehead gently with your fingertips.
  3. Work your way down around your eyes, cheeks, jawline and chin.
  4. Keep tapping in a light, steady rhythm to wake up circulation.

V Shape Release for Eyes (Right) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand comfortably.
  2. Make a "V" with the index and middle fingers of both hands.
  3. Use your left hand’s fingers to hold the right side of your face gently.
  4. With your right hand, press softly and glide your fingers upward toward your hairline.
  5. Keep the gliding motion smooth and continuous.

V Shape Release for Eyes (Left) — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand comfortably.
  2. Make a "V" with the index and middle fingers of both hands.
  3. Use your right hand’s fingers to hold the left side of your face gently.
  4. With your left hand, press softly and glide your fingers upward toward your hairline.
  5. Keep the gliding motion smooth and continuous.

Cheek Lifting and Sculpting — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Rest your palms on your cheekbones with your fingers loosely curled.
  3. Drop your jaw into a long "O" shape with your mouth.
  4. Press firmly but gently and lift your cheeks.
  5. Hold for a moment and feel the tension.

Bumblebee Lips — 1 minute 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand with a straight spine and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Breathe in deeply through your nose.
  3. Breathe out fully through your mouth with a lip trill — keep your lips loose so they vibrate together like a motorboat, a soft "brrr" sound.
  4. Keep breathing in through your nose and out through your mouth with the lip trill going.

Chin Lift Stretch — 1 minute 45 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall, shoulders relaxed, spine straight.
  2. Rest your hands flat on your shoulders.
  3. Lift your chin slowly toward the ceiling and look upward until you feel a gentle stretch in the front of your neck.
  4. Bring your head back to the start and repeat.

Cooldown

Cold Oil Seal Technique — 30 seconds

  1. Sit or stand tall with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Warm a little facial oil between your hands and press your palms softly onto your cheeks.
  3. Hold for a moment, then press your palms firmly over the centre of your face and lift them to cover your eyes gently.
  4. Repeat this calm palm press — cheeks, centre, eyes — breathing deeply as you relax.

How it should feel

A midday reset should leave your face tingly and awake, and the tapping should make your skin look a little brighter. Keep the taps light — never thumpy.
